# Northstar Technologies — Organizational Structure

**Document Classification:** Internal — HR & Operations  
**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Overview

Northstar Technologies operates a multi-disciplinary engineering organization designed to deliver world-class software products across artificial intelligence, financial technology, and core infrastructure domains. This document provides an authoritative reference to the current organizational structure within the Engineering Department and its constituent teams.

## Engineering Department

The Engineering Department serves as the backbone of Northstar Technologies' product development efforts. It is organized into three primary teams, each responsible for a distinct capability area: the AI Platform Team, the Payments Platform Team, and the Core Services Team. All three teams—the AI Platform Team, the Payments Platform Team, and the Core Services Team—are part of the Engineering Department and collaborate closely on cross-functional initiatives.

Bob Chen manages the AI Platform Team and is responsible for setting the technical direction for all artificial intelligence and machine learning initiatives at Northstar. Bob Chen reports to CTO Office, ensuring that the AI Platform Team's priorities remain tightly aligned with the company's executive technology strategy.

## Cross-Team Collaboration

Northstar Technologies encourages strong cross-team collaboration within the Engineering Department. Regular architecture review boards, shared on-call rotations, and unified incident response procedures ensure that the AI Platform Team, the Payments Platform Team, and the Core Services Team operate as a cohesive engineering organization despite their distinct areas of focus. Leadership across all three teams coordinates through biweekly engineering syncs chaired by the CTO Office.
